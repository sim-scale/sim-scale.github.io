Sim Scale / Research overview

SS

Scaling simulation diversity for zero-shot robot policy transfer.

Sim Scale builds realistic, diverse simulation assets and demonstrations to train policies that transfer directly to the physical world.

The evidence framework below separates generated data, simulation evaluation, and real-world rollouts. Placeholder panels remain explicitly labeled until reviewed media and metrics are available.

Project summary

Sim Scale explores whether scaling realistic asset diversity and demonstration coverage in simulation can produce generalist robot policies that transfer zero-shot to unseen physical assets. Results, media, and protocol details will be added only after review.
