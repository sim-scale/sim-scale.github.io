Sim Scale / Research overview

SS

Train on varied simulated assets. Test on unseen physical objects.

We generate assets and demonstrations in simulation, train a policy on that data, and test it on objects it has not seen before.

Simulation and real-world results are reported separately.

02 / Asset generation

Vary object geometry,
appearance, and physics.

For each object family, we vary shape, size, materials, mass, articulation, and starting pose.
